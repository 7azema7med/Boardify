USMLE Step 2 CK Cardiovascular
Item 1 of 3,420
A 62-year-old male presents to the emergency department with sudden, severe chest pain radiating to his interscapular region. Blood pressure is 185/105 mmHg in the right arm and 138/82 mmHg in the left arm. A grade 2/6 early diastolic murmur is heard along the right sternal border.
Which initial diagnostic test is most appropriate for this stable patient?
Key clinical takeaway: CT angiography of the chest is the initial diagnostic test of choice for suspected acute aortic dissection in hemodynamically stable patients. Blood pressure differential between arms and new aortic regurgitation murmur strongly support the diagnosis.

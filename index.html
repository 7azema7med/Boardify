A 62-year-old male presents with sudden-onset, tearing chest pain radiating to his interscapular back. Physical exam reveals a 3/6 decrescendo diastolic murmur at the right sternal border and pulse disparity across upper extremities.
Which initial diagnostic test is most appropriate for this hemodynamically stable patient?
Correct Choice (B): Contrast-enhanced CTA of the chest is the gold standard diagnostic test in hemodynamically stable patients with suspected Stanford Type A acute aortic dissection. Immediate IV beta-blockade is administered to lower aortic shear stress.
